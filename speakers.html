Featured Speakers

Dr. Sarah Nakato

Coffee Value Chain Researcher

National Agricultural Research Organisation, Uganda

Dr. Nakato researches practical ways to improve coffee productivity and quality. Her work focuses on connecting agricultural research with the needs of farming communities.

Prof. Daniel Okello

Agricultural Climate Scientist

Makerere University, Uganda

Professor Okello studies climate resilience and sustainable agricultural systems. He works with researchers and producers to strengthen adaptation across coffee-growing regions.

Grace Atim

Coffee Quality and Cupping Specialist

Uganda Coffee Development Authority, Uganda

Grace supports coffee quality improvement through training, evaluation and industry collaboration. She is passionate about helping Ugandan coffee reach and compete in specialty markets.

Michael Kato

Coffee Processing and Export Consultant

East African Coffee Advisory, Uganda

Michael advises coffee businesses on processing, value addition and export preparation. He helps enterprises identify opportunities to improve efficiency and reach new buyers.

Call for Papers

Researchers, practitioners and industry professionals are invited to submit original abstracts on coffee production, processing, sustainability, technology, trade and market development. Submissions should clearly state the topic, objectives, approach and key findings, and should be written in clear English.

Selected contributors may be invited to deliver a presentation during the expo. Submitters should provide their full name, organisation and contact details with their abstract; all submissions are subject to review by the fictional expo programme committee.
