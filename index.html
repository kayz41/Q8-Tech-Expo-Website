Connecting the coffee world

Celebrating Uganda's Coffee Excellence

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a fictional event organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). It brings together coffee farmers, processors, exporters, researchers, buyers and industry partners to celebrate Uganda's coffee sector and explore opportunities across the global coffee value chain.

Across three days at Speke Resort Munyonyo, participants will exchange knowledge, discover new products and technologies, build business relationships and discuss sustainable ways to grow the coffee industry. The programme features expert sessions, exhibitions and conversations about the future of coffee in Uganda and beyond.
